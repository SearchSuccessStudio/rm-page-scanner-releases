# Privacy Policy — RM Page Scanner

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

RM Page Scanner ("the app") is developed by Rashid Maqbool (Search Success Studio).

## What the app does with your data

- **Scans and documents** are created and stored **only on your phone**. We do not upload, collect or see your scans, PDFs or extracted text.
- **Camera** is used only to take photos of documents you choose to scan.
- **Photos / files** you import are read only to create scans inside the app.
- **Text recognition (OCR), QR / barcode reading and face detection** (for ID photos) run **on your device** using Google ML Kit. Your images are not sent to us.
- **Location** (optional) is used only if you turn on *Date & Location Stamp*, to print the place name on your own scans. It is not sent to us.
- **Internet** is used only to check whether a newer version of the app is available.
- **Backup** files are created only when you tap Backup, and are saved where you choose.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share any personal data. Files leave your phone only when you yourself share or export them (for example to WhatsApp or email).

## Third-party services

The app uses Google ML Kit and Google Play services, which run on your device and may send limited, anonymous diagnostic information to Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.
